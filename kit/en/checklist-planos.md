## Before You Start
- [ ] I know which plan I have (Settings › Plan).
- [ ] I created a **Project** with the instructions from step 1 (`contexto/instrucoes-do-projeto.md`).
- [ ] I gathered a brand source: PDF, screenshots of the website, or logo (step 2).
- [ ] I chose the first deliverable I make every week (proposal, report, post) to turn into a template.

## Before Sharing Any Deliverable
- [ ] No invented numbers, clients, or testimonials.
- [ ] For client materials, the logo belongs to THE CLIENT.
- [ ] Dashboard: I clicked the numbers and checked the query and refresh date.
- [ ] I shared only with people who need access (view or edit). Everything starts private.

## Export
PowerPoint, Google Slides (editable), PDF, MP4 (Motion), and HTML.
