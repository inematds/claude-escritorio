# Prompt 2 — your brand becomes a design system

**What a design system is:** the “brand guide” Claude keeps and uses on its own — colors, fonts, logo,
spacing, and image style. You set it up once; every piece you create afterward will look like your brand.

**What Claude needs (just one source is enough to get started):**
- a PDF or presentation for a brand you like;
- screenshots of the main pages on your site;
- your logo (preferably in light and dark versions) and the font names, if you know them;
- (optional, for developers) if your brand lives in code, the website repository.

**Where:** in a conversation (attach the files) or in Claude Settings, under **Design systems** (in the English app: Settings › Capabilities › Design systems › Manage).
The separate site claude.ai/design will close on Dec 14, 2026; if you have a design system there, migrate it through the Artifacts page.

```
I’ve attached [my brand PDF / screenshots of my site / my logo]. Create a design system for my brand that I can use
in documents, presentations, and design pieces.

1. Extract: the palette (with color codes and what each color is for), heading and body fonts, logo usage
   (light and dark backgrounds), spacing, corner styles, icon style, and photo/illustration style.
2. Show me a sample page: a presentation cover, a content slide, a square post, and a button.
3. List what you COULD NOT find in the files (e.g., “there’s no logo version for dark backgrounds”) and ask me
   before making anything up.
4. Write 5 short “do / don’t” rules for this brand.
```

**Review it once:** the design system will be approximate. Check the colors against the official codes (if available) and
correct anything that’s wrong by asking in the conversation (“the correct blue is #1E40AF”).

Example of what to check: `../design-system-modelo.md`.
