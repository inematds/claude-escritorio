# Prompt 3 — From Meeting to Proposal Slides (Claude Slides)

**When to use:** You’ve just finished a client meeting and need to send a proposal.  
**Where:** Inside your Project (step 1), with the design system (step 2). Artifacts › Slides tab, or ask in the conversation.  
**Plan:** Slides is available on all plans, including the free plan.

```
Paste the meeting notes (or transcript) with [CLIENT NAME] below.

Create a slide proposal using my design system:
1. First, show me the outline (one line with each slide’s title) and wait for my OK.
2. Research the public website [cliente.com.br]: what they sell, who they serve, and where they operate. List 3 questions
   I should still ask to finalize the proposal.
3. Cover slide with the CLIENT’S name and logo (not mine). If you can’t find the logo, leave a marked placeholder.
4. Structure: the problem in the client’s own words → what changes for them → how we do it (3 steps) →
   timeline → investment [use my table / leave blank] → next step with a date.
5. No more than 10 slides, one idea per slide, one chart or image per slide when it helps.
6. Write speaker notes for each slide.
7. Don’t make up numbers, case studies, or testimonials: use only what’s in my Project or the notes.

[PASTE THE NOTES HERE]
```

**Afterward:** Make edits directly on the slide or ask in the conversation (“combine slides 4 and 5,” “change the cover photo”).  
Present without leaving Claude, or export: Share/Export › PowerPoint, Google Slides (editable), or PDF.
