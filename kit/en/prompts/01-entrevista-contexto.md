# Prompt 1 — Claude interviews you and writes your context

**When to use:** First, if you don’t want to fill in the `contexto/instrucoes-do-projeto.md` template yourself.  
**Where:** In a new conversation. At the end, paste the result into **Project › Instructions**.

```
I want to create instructions for a Project in Claude so you can work as my office assistant
(documents, presentations, and design materials). Interview me.

Rules:
- Ask one short question at a time, and wait for my answer.
- Cover these topics in this order: who I am; my offer and pricing (I can say “don’t mention it”); my clients, their problem, and their objections; real proof (cases, testimonials—accept “none yet”); my way of speaking (tone, words I use and words I don’t); my brand (colors, fonts, logo, if I know).
- If I give a vague answer (“I work with everyone”), ask for a concrete example.
- Don’t make anything up. Leave out anything I don’t know.

When you’re done, write the instructions in this format, in English, in up to 400 words:
## Who I am / ## My offer / ## My clients / ## Proof / ## My way of speaking / ## Brand /
## Rules for Claude (include: don’t invent numbers, clients, testimonials, or prices; put the CLIENT’S logo on the cover of materials for clients; show the outline before creating a large piece; “like the last one” = use the last approved piece as a model).
```

**Afterward:** Projects › New project › paste the text into Instructions. Also upload 2 or 3 reference files (your sales presentation, a proposal that worked, the brand PDF).
