## What it is

A free kit for using Claude as your office: writing documents, building presentations, creating design pieces and, on some plans, data dashboards and animations. It is for people who work alone or in small teams and are tired of explaining their brand and business every time. The kit includes a "who I am" template to paste into a Claude Project, ready-made prompts for each tool, and a checklist of what each plan includes. All you need is a Claude account (the free one already covers Docs, Slides and Design) and a browser.

## The process, start to finish

| # | Step | What to do | Kit file |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Context | Projects › New project › paste the filled-in Instructions (or let Claude interview you) | `kit/en/contexto/instrucoes-do-projeto.md`, `kit/en/prompts/01-entrevista-contexto.md` |
| 2 | Brand | Attach a brand PDF, website screenshots or the logo and ask for the design system; check the colors | `kit/en/prompts/02-design-system.md`, `kit/en/design-system-modelo.md` |
| 3 | First template | Make the piece you repeat most (proposal, doc, post, dashboard, animation) | `kit/en/prompts/03-slides-proposta.md`, `04-docs-colega.md`, `05-design-peca.md`, `06-dashboard-numeros.md`, `07-motion-explicativo.md` |
| 4 | Reuse | The approved piece becomes a "Template for …" in the Project; the next ones come from it | `kit/en/prompts/08-reusar-modelo.md` |
| — | Before sharing | Check your plan, the numbers and who you share with | `kit/en/checklist-planos.md` |

## What each plan includes (Nov 4, 2026)

Docs, Slides and Design: every plan, including Free. Dashboards: beta, paid plans only. Motion: beta, Team and Enterprise only. Source: [claude.com/features/artifacts](https://claude.com/features/artifacts).
